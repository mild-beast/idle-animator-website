Idle Animator Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 4, 2026

Idle Animator is a mobile game made by Mild Beast Games LLC ("we", "us"). This policy explains what information the game collects, why, who receives it, and the choices you have. It applies to Idle Animator on iOS and Android.

The short version

  • You do not create an account, and we never ask for your name, email address or contacts.
  • Your game progress is saved on your device only.
  • We collect statistics about how the game is played, to improve it. You can turn this off in Settings.
  • If the game crashes, it sends us a technical report so we can fix the problem.
  • Ads are optional: you choose to watch one for a boost. Ads are personalized only if you allow it.
  • Purchases are handled by the App Store or Google Play. We never see your payment details.

What we collect and why

Gameplay statistics

Once you agree to this policy, the game sends statistics to Unity Analytics, a service run by Unity Technologies. We use them to understand how the game is played, balance it and find problems. The game reports these moments:

  • ranking up and which film you are working on;
  • claiming a mission reward;
  • unlocking or automating a job;
  • unlocking or upgrading a card, and opening a chest (including whether it was bought);
  • spending in-game currency on larger actions, with the amount and what it paid for;
  • returning after time away, with how long you were gone and what you earned;
  • starting a rewarded ad and how it ended, and the estimated revenue of an ad shown;
  • purchases made in the game, which the purchasing service reports.

Unity's software also records standard technical information with these statistics: a randomly generated identifier for your installation, device model, operating system version, language, app version, session times, and the country your connection appears to come from (derived from your IP address).

You can stop this at any time: open Settings in the game and turn off "Share gameplay statistics".

Advertising

The game offers rewarded ads: you choose to watch one in exchange for a boost. Ads are delivered through Unity LevelPlay (formerly ironSource) and the Unity Ads network, both run by Unity Technologies. To deliver and measure ads, these services receive technical information such as your IP address, device model, operating system, language, and which ads you were shown and how you interacted with them.

The first time you play, you are asked whether our ad partners may use data from your device, such as its advertising identifier, to show ads that suit you. On Android the game asks this itself. On iOS it is asked through Apple's tracking permission prompt.

  • If you allow it, ad partners may use your advertising identifier to personalize ads.
  • If you decline, we tell our ad partners that you have not consented, and you still get the same game with ads that are not personalized.

You can change your answer at any time under "Personalized ads" in Settings. On iOS, if you declined Apple's prompt, turning it back on takes you to your device's settings, where tracking is allowed.

Crash reports

If the game crashes or hits an error, it sends a report to Unity's crash reporting service so we can find and fix the problem. A report contains what the game was doing when it failed (the error and a short log of the game's recent technical messages), together with the device model, operating system version, app version, memory and graphics details, and a randomly generated identifier for your installation. It does not contain your name or contact details. Crash reports are sent whenever the game is running and are not affected by the "Share gameplay statistics" setting.

Purchases

In-app purchases are processed by Apple's App Store or Google Play under their own privacy policies. We receive confirmation of what was bought so the game can deliver it. We never receive your card number or other payment details.

Your saved game and settings

Your progress, sound settings and privacy choices are stored on your device. They are not sent to us. Deleting the game deletes them.

What we do not collect

The game does not collect your name, email address, phone number, contacts, photos, precise location, or anything from your microphone or camera.

Your choices

In the game, open Settings and look under "Ads and privacy":

  • Personalized ads: allow or withdraw permission for ad partners to personalize ads.
  • Share gameplay statistics: turn the collection of gameplay statistics on or off.
  • Do not sell or share my data: tell our ad partners not to sell or share your information, as provided by US state privacy laws such as the CCPA.

You can also reset or limit your advertising identifier in your device settings: on iOS under Privacy & Security > Tracking, and on Android under Privacy > Ads.

Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to ask for a copy of the information held about you, to have it corrected or deleted, to object to or restrict its use, and to withdraw consent you have given. If you live in the European Economic Area or the United Kingdom, you may also complain to your local data protection authority. To make a request, contact us at the address below. Because the game has no accounts, we may need details from your device to find your information.

Where the law requires a legal basis, we rely on your consent for personalized ads, on your agreement to this policy and our legitimate interest in improving the game for gameplay statistics, on our legitimate interest in keeping the game working for crash reports, and on performing our agreement with you for delivering purchases.

How long information is kept

Gameplay statistics, crash reports and advertising records are kept by Unity for as long as needed for the purposes above and in line with Unity's retention practices, then deleted or made anonymous. Information saved on your device stays there until you delete the game.

International transfers

Unity and the other recipients listed above may process information in countries other than your own, including the United States. They use safeguards such as standard contractual clauses where required.

Children

Idle Animator is not directed at children under 13 (or the higher age your country sets for consenting to data processing), and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them. If you believe a child has provided personal information through the game, contact us and we will have it deleted.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as the game changes. The date at the top shows the latest version. If a change is significant, the game will ask you to agree to the updated policy.
